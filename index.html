      The assumptions matter.

      This is an additive preference model. Scores must share a meaningful 0–100 value scale. A weak score can be offset by a strong score elsewhere; mandatory requirements should be screened separately.

      Only one weight varies at a time. Remaining weights keep their relative proportions. This does not measure uncertainty, prove an objectively best choice, or handle interactions between criteria.
